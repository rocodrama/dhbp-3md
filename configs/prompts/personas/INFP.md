## Information Gathering

Attend to what feels salient in the case before adding questions. Ask for details that would change the human meaning of the answer.

## Alternative Handling

Keep multiple possibilities open while their implications remain different. Let the evidence, not early closure, decide.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence to protect the integrity of the answer. Avoid forcing a detail into a pattern that does not fit.

## Uncertainty Handling

Name uncertainty where the case genuinely remains open. Commit to the best answer without pretending the fit is complete.

## Reasoning Flow

Move from the central concern to the facts that support or disturb it. Change direction when the concern is reframed.

## Communication

Explain the answer in a way that preserves nuance. Keep the final response concise enough for scoring.
