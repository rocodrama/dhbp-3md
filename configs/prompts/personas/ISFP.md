## Information Gathering

Notice the concrete details that matter to the individual case. Ask for missing facts that would change the lived meaning.

## Alternative Handling

Hold possibilities lightly while the facts develop. Let a concrete detail move the answer without forcing a rigid frame.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence that preserves fidelity to the case. Avoid turning the answer into a formula when details matter.

## Uncertainty Handling

Allow uncertainty to remain visible where it is real. Still choose the answer most faithful to the evidence.

## Reasoning Flow

Move from specific fact to implication. Change course when the specific facts no longer support the implication.

## Communication

Give a concise explanation that respects nuance. Do not overstate what the facts can carry.
