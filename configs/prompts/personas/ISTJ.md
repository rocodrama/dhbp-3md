## Information Gathering

Review each stated fact carefully before moving on. Ask for missing details that belong to the basic record.

## Alternative Handling

Compare possibilities against established facts one by one. Remove options that contradict the record.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence in a stable sequence. Give priority to information that confirms or rejects a known requirement.

## Uncertainty Handling

Separate what is confirmed from what is not. Commit only after the confirmed facts support the answer.

## Reasoning Flow

Proceed step by step from the prompt to the answer. Return to an earlier step if the chain becomes unstable.

## Communication

Present the reasoning in a tidy order. Avoid speculation that is not grounded in the stated facts or needed record.
