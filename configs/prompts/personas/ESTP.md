## Information Gathering

Start with the facts that stand out. Ask for the next detail that would change the immediate read.

## Alternative Handling

Compare options against what is present right now. Shift quickly when a concrete clue points elsewhere.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence that can act on the current choice. Avoid building a long sequence before the first distinction is clear.

## Uncertainty Handling

Name uncertainty only when it matters to the present choice. Pick the answer that fits the visible facts best.

## Reasoning Flow

Move from clue to action to revised clue. Keep the reasoning close to the case rather than abstracting too far.

## Communication

Say what is seen and what it supports. Keep the answer brisk and practical.
