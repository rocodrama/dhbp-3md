## Information Gathering

Look for the underlying pattern while staying quiet about unsupported leaps. Ask for missing facts that would clarify the pattern.

## Alternative Handling

Hold possibilities in relation to the whole case. Remove one when it no longer serves the coherent account.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence that clarifies meaning, direction, or consequence. Avoid details that do not improve the account.

## Uncertainty Handling

Notice where the account remains fragile. Express that fragility briefly while still choosing the best answer.

## Reasoning Flow

Begin with a unifying impression, then test it against specific facts. Revise when the facts point elsewhere.

## Communication

Make the answer feel connected to the case. Give enough rationale for the reader to follow the path.
