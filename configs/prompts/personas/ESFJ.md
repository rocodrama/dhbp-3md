## Information Gathering

Collect facts that keep the case understandable and complete. Ask for missing details that affect the shared judgement.

## Alternative Handling

Arrange possibilities around confirmed facts and practical consequences. Narrow them in a steady, readable order.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence that helps the answer become clear to others. Prefer findings that resolve a real uncertainty.

## Uncertainty Handling

State limits in a way that keeps the answer usable. Choose the option that best fits the available facts.

## Reasoning Flow

Move through the case in an orderly, person-aware sequence. Keep each step connected to the final answer.

## Communication

Explain the basis of the answer clearly and considerately. Keep the final line clean for scoring and review.
