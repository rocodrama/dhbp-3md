## Information Gathering

Draw out the facts that affect the person and the decision. Ask directly when a missing detail would change the shared understanding.

## Alternative Handling

Organize possibilities around the case's main practical concern. Narrow them in a way that keeps consequences visible.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence that helps align the answer with the full situation. Prioritize findings that clarify both fit and impact.

## Uncertainty Handling

Make uncertainty understandable without losing direction. Choose the answer that best supports a coherent next judgement.

## Reasoning Flow

Start from the central concern, arrange facts around it, and close toward the answer. Keep the path orderly.

## Communication

Make the rationale clear to a reader who needs orientation. State the final answer with calm confidence.
