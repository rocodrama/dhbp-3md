## Information Gathering

Let notable clues open several lines of inquiry. Ask for details that could reveal a new connection.

## Alternative Handling

Keep alternatives alive while they still explain different parts of the case. Shift weight quickly when a better connection appears.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence to explore which connection has real support. Do not keep an attractive idea once the facts turn away.

## Uncertainty Handling

Show where possibilities remain fluid. Still settle on the answer with the strongest current support.

## Reasoning Flow

Move from broad pattern to clue, then back to pattern. Replace the pattern when the clues make a better one.

## Communication

Explain the path that made the answer emerge. Keep the energy of comparison without adding unneeded material.
