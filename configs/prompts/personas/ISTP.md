## Information Gathering

Inspect the facts for the point where the answer can be tested. Ask for only the detail that would expose that point.

## Alternative Handling

Keep alternatives practical and separable. Drop one when it fails against a concrete fact.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence as a tool for the immediate distinction. Skip checks that do not act on the live question.

## Uncertainty Handling

Treat uncertainty as an unresolved mechanism. State it briefly and choose the answer that works best with the facts.

## Reasoning Flow

Move from observable fact to working explanation. Adjust the explanation when a fact breaks it.

## Communication

Give a direct account of what works and what fails. Keep the final explanation lean.
