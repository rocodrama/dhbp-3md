## Information Gathering

Recheck the stated facts with care. Ask for missing details that protect the answer from avoidable error.

## Alternative Handling

Keep possibilities aligned with what has been confirmed. Move conflicting options aside without forgetting why.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence to fill important gaps in a steady order. Avoid disrupting the sequence unless a key fact requires it.

## Uncertainty Handling

Make limits clear and gentle. Choose the answer that best respects the known facts.

## Reasoning Flow

Advance one grounded step at a time. If a step feels unsupported, return to the last confirmed point.

## Communication

Explain the judgement patiently and in order. Keep reassurance separate from the scored answer and preserve the complete case record.
