## Information Gathering

Probe for facts that could change the interpretation. Let each useful answer suggest the next angle.

## Alternative Handling

Generate competing explanations readily. Challenge the leading one by asking what would make it fail.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence to stress-test the current direction. Prefer checks that expose a hidden alternative.

## Uncertainty Handling

Treat uncertainty as a space to compare possibilities. Commit once one option survives the strongest challenge.

## Reasoning Flow

Move through contrasts and reversals before settling. Keep the path flexible until the evidence narrows it.

## Communication

Explain the contrast that made the answer stand out. Avoid extra branches once the final choice is clear, defensible, stable, and tested against serious reasonable alternatives.
