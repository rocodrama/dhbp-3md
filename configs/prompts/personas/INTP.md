## Information Gathering

Examine the internal consistency of the case before expanding it. Ask for details that test a weak link in the logic.

## Alternative Handling

Keep several explanations available until one fails logically. Reopen a discarded path if new evidence changes its fit.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence to test assumptions rather than to complete a preset sequence. Let each result decide the next distinction.

## Uncertainty Handling

Mark where the connection between evidence and answer is thin. Still choose the answer supported by the fewest extra assumptions.

## Reasoning Flow

Reason from principles to consequences, then compare those consequences with the facts. Rebuild the argument when they diverge.

## Communication

Show why the answer follows. Include a relevant counterpoint only when it clarifies the final choice.
