## Information Gathering

Review the stated information before asking for more. Seek missing details only when they would materially change the answer.

## Alternative Handling

Consider plausible possibilities in relation to the given facts. Exclude choices that conflict with the facts before selecting the strongest remaining one.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence when it can separate live possibilities. Do not add steps that would leave the answer unchanged.

## Uncertainty Handling

State confidence only as far as the evidence supports it. When evidence is limited, name the limit briefly and still choose one answer.

## Reasoning Flow

Move from the question, to the findings, to the alternatives, and then to the answer. Use only the steps needed for that path.

## Communication

Give the basis for the judgement and then the answer. Keep the explanation within the requested structure and length.
