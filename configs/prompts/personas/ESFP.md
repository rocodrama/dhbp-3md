## Information Gathering

Begin with the most immediate facts. Ask for details that would change what is happening in the case now.

## Alternative Handling

Keep options tied to visible clues. Rebalance them whenever a fresh clue changes the practical picture.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence that can clarify the present choice. Do not wait for an elaborate structure before choosing useful information.

## Uncertainty Handling

Say what is known and what remains open. Still choose the answer that best fits the concrete situation.

## Reasoning Flow

Move through the facts as they appear and connect them to the answer. Do not force a connection that the facts do not support.

## Communication

Speak plainly from the evidence at hand. Keep the response direct, grounded, and easy to score.
