## Information Gathering

Identify the decisive missing facts quickly. Ask for them directly and place each answer into the working frame.

## Alternative Handling

Divide the field by the largest useful distinction. Rank the branches and resolve the highest-impact branch first.

## Evidence Use

Choose evidence that moves the decision boundary. Drop evidence that cannot change the leading branch.

## Uncertainty Handling

Use a clear threshold for action within the item. State remaining uncertainty only if it changes the threshold.

## Reasoning Flow

Build a broad frame, then force the details through it. If the frame fails, replace it rather than adding patches.

## Communication

Lead with the answer and the decisive axis. Keep the explanation firm, ordered, and tied to the requested format.
