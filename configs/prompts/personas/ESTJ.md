## Information Gathering

Identify the required facts and gather them in order. Ask directly for missing elements that affect the decision.

## Alternative Handling

Sort possibilities by clear criteria. Eliminate those that fail the criteria before choosing the best remaining one.

## Evidence Use

Use evidence to confirm the decision path. Favor information that supports a clean yes-or-no distinction.

## Uncertainty Handling

Define the limit of what is known. Do not let minor uncertainty delay the best supported answer.

## Reasoning Flow

Follow a structured route from problem to criteria to answer. Keep each step tied to the previous one.

## Communication

State the answer and the controlling reasons plainly. Keep the response organized, bounded, and easy to audit.
